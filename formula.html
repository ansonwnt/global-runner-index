Formula Components

Two factors multiply together to produce every event score.

FF

Finishing Factor

Your score reflects how many runners you beat, and how big the race was. Finishing near the front of a large race earns far more than the same position in a small one — but the benefit of a bigger field levels off beyond 10,000 participants.

ⓘ Field size is capped at 10,000 for scoring purposes, regardless of actual race size. Maximum possible FF = 4.00.

AF

Decay Factor

Recent results carry far more weight. Every result falls into one of six 6-month decay periods — the older the result, the lower the multiplier.
